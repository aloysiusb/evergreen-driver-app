Live Route Map
Driver app
Drivers tap the Map tab and see their entire route plotted — every stop, in order, on a live Google Map with real-time traffic. No address entry, no setup. The route is already there.
✅
Stops are color-coded to match your Google Sheet row colors — so drivers instantly recognize accounts they know.
⚡
Planned vs. Live Route
Driver app
Two modes, one tap to switch:

📋 Planned — follows the order in your Google Sheet exactly, shown in green.
⚡ Live — recalculates the remaining stops using real-time traffic to find the fastest order, shown in amber.
🕐
Live mode shows estimated time saved vs. the planned order — drivers can decide if the optimization is worth it that day.
🚦
Live Traffic & Reroute Alerts
Driver app
The route bar shows real congestion, not just an ETA — comparing current traffic against free-flowing conditions so drivers know why they're running behind.
🟢 Light traffic 🟡 Moderate · +11 min 🔴 Heavy · +24 min
⚡
Every 3 minutes the app quietly re-checks traffic. If conditions shift enough that reordering would save 8+ minutes, the driver gets a nudge with a Switch button — it never reroutes on its own.
🚦
This is the same live traffic data that powers Waze — road closures, accidents, and congestion reported by drivers feed straight into your route planning.
🛰️
Full Maps & Turn-by-Turn Handoff
Driver app
Every Google Maps capability is available: satellite and hybrid view to spot driveways and bin placement before pulling up, Street View to scout unfamiliar properties, terrain, and full zoom and rotate controls.
↗️
Tapping Navigate hands off to whichever app the driver prefers — Google Maps, Waze, or Apple Maps — with the stop's exact coordinates, not a fuzzy address search.
📍
Real-Time GPS Navigation
Driver app
A blue dot tracks the driver's live position on the map. A card at the bottom of the screen shows the current stop — name, address, any special notes — and a live ETA badge that updates as they drive.
🚗
When the driver pulls within 80 metres of their next stop, the app gently prompts: "Arrived? ✓ Done" — one tap marks it complete and moves to the next stop automatically.
↗️
The Navigate button hands off to Google Maps for turn-by-turn — so drivers get the best of both worlds: your route data here, Google's navigation there.
🔑
Gate Codes & Access Notes
Driver + Dispatch
Whatever dispatch types in the Driving Directions column of the sheet — gate codes, bin colors, driveway notes, "ring the bell" — now shows as a visible blue chip on every stop card and in the navigation view. Drivers never have to search for it.
📋
Dispatch updates the sheet → drivers see it instantly, no app update needed.
📋
2-Tap Incident Reports
Driver + Dispatch
Four quick buttons appear on every stop. Tap one, answer one follow-up question, done. The report saves directly to the Google Sheet as a structured note — no phone calls, no paper, no guessing later what happened.
🚫 Contamination 📦 Overflow ⏭ Skip 📷 Photo
🔍
Each type drills down to specifics — contamination asks what kind (black bags, recyclables, food waste…) and how much. Overflow asks severity and what the driver did. Skip asks the reason. Every report is traceable.
📷
Photo reports open the phone camera directly. The note records that a photo was taken for follow-up.
