What's live today

The driver app — running now

Each driver gets a color-coded route on their phone. No paper, no spreadsheet hunting. Everything talks back to the Google Sheet the office already uses.

📱

Per-driver route view

Each route has its own color scheme, its own URL, and only shows that driver's stops for the day.

✅

Real-time done/undo

Tap to mark a stop complete — it writes back to the Google Sheet instantly. Tap Undo if it was accidental.

🔄

Live sync across devices

If the office changes a note or Alyssa updates the sheet, drivers see it in seconds — no refresh needed.

📋

Daily backup

"Start My Day" snapshots the route before any changes, so there's always a clean original to reference.

🗺️

Map + directions built in

Every stop has a Map button that opens Google Maps, and driving directions pulled directly from the sheet.

🎨

Sheet colors preserved

Orange, purple, and other highlight colors from the route sheet carry through to the app — the legend still works.

The bigger picture

Making the day a little easier

I'd love to pick your brains — what's the biggest headache day to day? These are just some areas I've been thinking about, but you and your team are the ones who really know where things get heavy. That's where I'd want to focus.

Customer requests

Holds, missed pickups, address changes — what if customers could handle those themselves with a simple link?

Driver updates

Notes and skips from the road, visible to the office in real time — no phone tag in the middle of a route.

New customer flow

Website inquiries that find their way to the right place automatically, with a confirmation already sent.

End-of-day summary

A quick digest of what happened on each route — skips, notes, flags — without anyone having to compile it.

How we'd approach it together

None of this has to happen at once. The driver app is already running — everything else can be added one piece at a time, only where it actually helps. The goal is just to make the information flow to the right person without someone having to carry it there manually.

What's possible next

Automation opportunities

Each of these builds on what's already in place and can be phased in one at a time.

1

Starlink + dashcam fleet

With Starlink on each truck and a dashcam, the driver app becomes a live operations dashboard. GPS location, stop confirmation with timestamp, and camera footage tied to each route. Alyssa can see where every truck is without a phone call — and disputes about pickups have video evidence.

Fleet visibility Dispute resolution Driver accountability
2

Website form → auto-routing

New customer submissions on the site get automatically added to a staging list, sent a confirmation email, and flagged for Alyssa to approve — without her manually forwarding anything. An approval click adds them to the correct route tab in the sheet.

Less inbox triaging No lost leads Faster onboarding
3

Driver notes → office log

Notes drivers add in the app (skip, hold, access issue, damaged bin) are already stored in real time. This can be extended to a daily summary that emails Alyssa each evening — what was skipped, what needs follow-up, what complaints came in — so nothing falls through the cracks overnight.

Already partially built Daily digest email Searchable history
4

QuickBooks automation

The QuickBooks API can do a lot more than just send payment links. Once connected, it can handle the billing side of the business almost entirely on its own:

  • Payment self-service — customers enter their email and get their own personalized invoice link to pay or set up autopay. No phone call, no manual send.
  • Auto-create invoices — when a new customer signs up, their invoice is created in QBO automatically.
  • Send invoice emails on demand — trigger QBO to email a customer their invoice without Alyssa opening QuickBooks.
  • Recurring billing — set up monthly invoices to generate automatically for every active customer.
  • Overdue alerts — pull a list of who's 30/60/90 days past due and send a reminder automatically.

The infrastructure is already built and ready to connect once QuickBooks is authorized — one conversation with Alyssa to click through the approval.

Fewer payment calls Autopay & recurring billing Works with existing QBO Auto invoicing
5

Customer self-service portal

A simple page where customers can update their address, request a hold, or flag a missed pickup — without calling the office. Requests appear in the sheet as pending changes for Alyssa to confirm with one click. This is the single highest-leverage thing for reducing inbound calls.

Fewer phone calls Customer satisfaction Route accuracy
